Company Overview

Amend2 is a U.S.-based manufacturer of firearm accessories, magazines, grips, and other non-regulated components. Known for durable polymer magazines and distinctive designs, the company serves a large community of firearm enthusiasts, collectors, and sport shooters, selling primarily through e-commerce and retail partners. As the market matured, Amend2 looked for ways to deepen customer engagement and build more predictable revenue beyond one-time purchases.

THE CHALLENGE

Revenue Volatility from One-Time Purchases

Like most manufacturers selling physical products online, Amend2 lived with revenue volatility. Nearly every sale was a one-time purchase driven by a promotion, a product launch, or seasonal demand, which made forecasting a guessing game.

  • Revenue tied to promotions and launches, with unpredictable demand cycles
  • A highly engaged base that bought again and again, but with no recurring structure to capture it
  • Strong enthusiasm for limited-edition designs, with no way to monetize it predictably
  • Recurring billing and monthly renewals across different products that would be unmanageable by hand
THE SOLUTION

Collectible Subscription Program with Rebillia

Amend2 partnered with Rebillia to launch a subscription program built around a new limited-edition magazine design every month. Subscribers get priority access before the general public, turning collectible enthusiasm into both exclusivity and predictable recurring demand.
