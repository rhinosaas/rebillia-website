RETENTION

Retain More Customers, Automatically

Most lost subscription revenue comes from preventable cancellations. Rebillia fights back on every front, smarter cancel flows, win-back offers, backup ways to charge a declined card, and early action on at-risk customers.

Smart Cancel Flows

Understand why a customer is leaving and offer the right bundle, product, plan, discount, pause, downgrade, or skip.

Win-Back Offers

Follow the client's changing needs, automatically reach out to canceled customers with personalized, well-timed comeback offers.

Intelligent Retry & Failover

Card declined? Rebillia retries at the right times and routes through a backup processor to recover the revenue.

Proactive Churn Prevention

Spot customers likely to leave before they cancel, and act early, with offers, outreach, or plan changes.
