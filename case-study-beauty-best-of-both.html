HOW IT WORKS

Billing Built Around the Parts That Actually Change

Instead of treating a subscription as one fixed product on one fixed interval, Rebillia breaks it into independent parts: product, shade, quantity, cadence, price, payment method, and customer preferences. Each part can be adjusted mid-subscription without cancelling, recreating the order, or losing the customer's history.

A subscriber can swap a shade, change a formula, add a targeted product, adjust cadence to match consumption, update their payment method, or pair with a complementary brand while the underlying subscription stays intact. For beauty brands, that turns subscription management from a support burden into infrastructure that protects the customer relationship through the normal changes that used to end it.

IN PRACTICE

How This Applies to Beauty Brands

Consider a beauty brand selling makeup, skincare, sun protection, or targeted treatments. A typical subscriber might start with one shade or formula. Then seasons change. They travel and need different products. They discover a new concern and want to add something. A shade needs adjustment as their skin tone shifts. They want to pair their go-to product with another brand's complementary formula.

With a rigid subscription system, each of these moments forces friction: contact the brand, wait for manual adjustment, create a separate order, or cancel and restart. With Rebillia, the customer makes simple changes through self-service, or the brand makes adjustments in seconds without losing continuity, and can even partner with other brands to sell bundled subscriptions. This pattern applies across beauty, whether customers are managing seasonal shade changes, formula swaps for skin sensitivity, routine adjustments, multi-product stacks, or partner brand pairings.

STANDOUT FEATURE

Partner Subscriptions: Selling Better Together

Rebillia also supports a feature that's especially powerful for beauty: two brands selling a joint subscription together, billed as one relationship instead of two separate checkouts.

Picture a moisturizer brand and a mineral sunscreen brand. On their own, a customer manages two separate subscriptions and two billing relationships. Partnered on Rebillia, the same customer can subscribe once, on either brand's storefront, and receive both products on a cadence that fits how they actually use them, while each brand keeps its own catalog, pricing, and payment setup behind the scenes.

For the brands, that means a bigger basket, a shared audience, and a genuinely complementary product experience without either one needing to build custom infrastructure just to make the partnership work.
